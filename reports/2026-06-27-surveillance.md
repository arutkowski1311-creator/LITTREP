### 1. Executive Summary
A near-silent window for LITT itself. No new LITT outcomes, epilepsy-ablation studies, FDA
LITT/neuro clearances, coverage decisions, or glioma trials verified inside 2026-04-27 → 2026-06-27.

  to LITT's non-resectable/deep/recurrent niche.

  trial tested *timing*, not efficacy.

### Domain: Adjacent & Competitive Therapies

#### ROADS — Cs-131 tile brachytherapy (GammaTile) vs postoperative SRS, resected brain mets
- **Citation:** Weinberg J, et al. ROADS phase 3 RCT. ASCO 2026, LBA2000; J Clin Oncol 44 (2026), suppl 16.
- **Date:** 2026-05-30 · **Venue:** ASCO 2026 (late-breaking oral)
- **Design:** Multicenter randomized (1:1) phase 3, 32 centers, 2021–2025.
- **Population / N:** Newly diagnosed operable brain mets 2–7 cm requiring resection; ~204 randomized (some materials cite 230 enrolled — denominator pending).
- **Endpoints:** Surgical-bed recurrence + OS (co-primary); SB-RFS, RN, QoL.
- **Results:** 12-mo surgical-bed recurrence ~1.0–1.3% vs ~11.9–15.4% (~94% relative reduction); SB-RFS HR 0.48 (p=0.002); 24-mo OS 61.7% vs 35.7%, HR 0.59 (p=0.032); no excess toxicity/RN.
- **Significance / limitations:** Met endpoints; OS p=0.032 borderline; industry-sponsored; abstract-stage; statistics vary by outlet (recurrence HR cited 0.07 in one source). 
- **Clinical bottom line:** Intraoperative Cs-131 tiles cut cavity recurrence ~10-fold with an OS signal vs post-op SRS in operable mets — potentially practice-shaping if confirmed.
- **Clinical Impact:** ★★★★ · **LITT Business Impact:** 6 / **headwind** · **Levers:** competitive, referral · **Horizon:** ≤12mo
- **Indications:** brain_met, radiation_necrosis · **Strategies:** comparative_effectiveness, srs_recurrence_salvage, radiation_necrosis_salvage
- **Who should know:** Medical Affairs, Marketing, Exec, Sales · **Provenance:** partial

#### NEO-TACTICS — neoadjuvant fractionated SRT then resection, brain mets
- **Citation:** Mitsuya K, et al. J Clin Oncol 44 (2026), suppl 16, abstr 2003; ASCO 2026.
- **Date:** 2026-05-30 · **Venue:** ASCO 2026 (oral)
- **Design:** Multicenter (14 centers) single-arm phase II · **N:** 55 evaluable.
- **Population:** Resectable met 2–5 cm (±≤3 non-index <2 cm); FSRT 30–35 Gy/5 fx then resection.
- **Results:** Local recurrence 4.3% (6 mo) / 16.4% (12 mo); intracranial PFS 72.5% / 64.4%; zero LMD; low RN; favorable cognition.
- **Limitations:** Single-arm, N=55, no control; needs phase 3.
- **Clinical bottom line:** Preop FSRT before resection gives excellent local control with no LMD and low necrosis — reinforces preop-SRS for resectable mets.
- **Clinical Impact:** ★★★ · **LITT Business Impact:** 4 / **headwind** · **Levers:** competitive · **Horizon:** 2-5yr
- **Indications:** brain_met, radiation_necrosis · **Provenance:** verified

#### TRIDENT — TTFields at chemoradiation start vs maintenance start, newly diagnosed GBM
- **Citation:** Novocure topline release, 18 Jun 2026; full data accepted ASTRO 2026.
- **Date:** 2026-06-18 · **Venue:** Company topline (full data ASTRO 2026)
- **Design:** Phase 3 randomized (both arms receive TTFields; timing differs) · **N:** 981.
- **Endpoint:** OS (primary).
- **Results:** Primary **MISSED** — median OS 17.7 vs 17.5 mo, no significant difference; no new safety signals; early start feasible. (Stock −~20%.)
- **Limitations:** Topline only; no TTFields-free control — informs timing, not efficacy.
- **Clinical bottom line:** Earlier Optune start does not improve survival; GBM positioning unchanged.
- **Clinical Impact:** ★★★ · **LITT Business Impact:** 4 / **tailwind** (relative) · **Levers:** competitive, messaging · **Horizon:** now
- **Indications:** newly_gbm · **Provenance:** verified

#### NeoVax — personalized neoantigen vaccine + pembrolizumab, newly diagnosed GBM
- **Citation:** Reardon DA, Wu CJ, et al. J Clin Oncol 44 (2026), suppl 16, abstr 2006; ASCO 2026 (Dana-Farber).
- **Date:** 2026-05-30 · **Venue:** ASCO 2026 (CNS oral)
- **Design:** Single-arm phase 1 (4 cohorts) vs propensity-matched historical controls · **N:** 39 enrolled, 37 treated.
- **Results:** Neoantigen-specific T cells with intracranial trafficking (>1 yr in some); median OS 36.9 mo (MGMT-methylated) / 19.0 mo (unmethylated) vs matched 25.3 / 16.7 mo; pembro-before-priming linked to longer OS.
- **Limitations:** Phase 1, N=39, historical controls, small subgroups, selection bias.
- **Clinical bottom line:** Feasible, immunogenic, provocative survival signal — promising, not practice-changing.
- **Clinical Impact:** ★★★ · **LITT Business Impact:** 3 / **neutral** · **Levers:** pipeline, messaging · **Horizon:** 2-5yr
- **Indications:** newly_gbm · **Strategies:** litt_immuno_combo · **Provenance:** verified
